# Public cultural information website

Status: Implemented concept for consultant review. Client identity, product records, address, opening hours, contact details, and legal wording remain unverified placeholders.

## Purpose

Create an attractive public website that communicates culture and invites exploration while remaining informational. The first phase takes priority over the proposed staff and administration portal.

## Experience

The design uses an after-dark editorial-collage direction: bold condensed typography, electric chartreuse, ultraviolet and burnt orange, print grain, original abstract botanical art, and restrained motion. The page is responsive and usable by keyboard.

The visitor journey is:

1. Confirm the adult-audience notice.
2. See all five product-format categories immediately in the first viewport.
3. Open a category guide from its dark editorial card.
4. Learn which verified details should accompany product information.
5. Discover the proposed culture desk and local creative direction.
6. Find the store-information section and a general-contact explanation.

## Included

- Single-page responsive cultural information experience
- Adult-audience entry notice
- Five interactive format explanations presented on the landing screen
- Factual label-information guidance
- Original hero and editorial artwork
- Placeholder location, hours, and general-contact content
- General WhatsApp integration concept without a live number or order capture

## Boundaries

The implementation contains no prices, stock status, cart, checkout, order form, quantities, delivery workflow, product recommendations, medical claims, or purchase-oriented WhatsApp message. The client must supply and approve all identity, product, location, contact, warning, and authorisation information before public launch.

## Required client inputs

- Approved business name, logo, brand assets, and tone
- Exact address, opening hours, contact number, and accessibility details
- Verified product entries and supporting documents
- Approved warnings and legal wording from qualified advisers
- Permission and attribution details for any local art or editorial content
- Written approval of the final public content

## Later phase

The owner and staff administration workspace is documented separately in `SPEC.md` and `DEMO-BRIEF.md`. It should be reconsidered only after discovery identifies a real administrative problem.
