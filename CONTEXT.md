# Client Services

The commercial relationship through which the consultant provides technology and operational support to a client business.

## Language

**Consultant**:
The independent service provider proposing and delivering agreed work for the client business.
_Avoid_: Business owner, employee

**Client**:
The business engaging the consultant, distinct from the people who purchase that business's products or services.
_Avoid_: Customer

**Project fee**:
A charge for a defined body of work with agreed deliverables and completion criteria.
_Avoid_: Monthly service fee

**Monthly service fee**:
A recurring charge for an agreed scope of ongoing services during each month.
_Avoid_: Unlimited support, profit share
